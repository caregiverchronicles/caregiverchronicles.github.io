Why Caregiver Chronicles exists

Hi, I'm Medha Kannan, a high school sophomore and the founder of Caregiver Chronicles. I started this page to spread awareness and share tips for caregivers, especially those caring for loved ones with neurological conditions like Parkinson's, dementia, seizures, and aphasia.

Through my IRB-approved research on caregiver burden at a neurology office, I've heard from caregivers about the patience, exhaustion, love, and loneliness that come with this role. Much of that work happens behind the scenes, and I want people to see it.

Their words, shared anonymously and with permission, are on the Caregiver voices page. If you're a caregiver, I hope you find something here that makes you feel a little less alone.

Follow @caregiver.chronicles on TikTok

If caregiving feels like too much right now, you don't have to carry it alone.

Call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) any time. The Family Caregiver Alliance at caregiver.org also has resources and support groups.
