Share your story

Your experience matters

Answer one question, a few, or all of them. Every question is optional, and you never need to share your name.

  • What aspects of caregiving have caused you the greatest amount of stress?
  • What do you wish others understood about the realities of caregiving?
  • How has becoming a caregiver changed your everyday/social life, responsibilities, or relationships?
  • In what ways has caregiving changed how you view yourself?
  • What advice would you give to others who have started caregiving for a loved one with a neurological disorder?
  • How has caregiving affected your emotional well-being over time?
  • If you could change one thing about the support caregivers receive, what would it be?
  • What has surprised you the most about the caregiving experience?
  • What emotions do you experience most often as a caregiver?
  • If you could describe your caregiving journey in one word or phrase, what would it be?

If caregiving feels like too much right now, you don't have to carry it alone.

Call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) any time. The Family Caregiver Alliance at caregiver.org also has resources and support groups.
